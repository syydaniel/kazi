# 英文克隆参考的朗读稿（tools/ref_prep.py --script 用）。一句一行，[P#] 开新段。225 个词，约 80 秒，录法见 docs/02_配音.md「英文片」。
[P1]
Hi there.
Have you ever watched an explainer video and thought, something about this feels a little off?
The pictures look fine, the voice sounds fine, but the two just don't line up.
That's the problem I've been working on for the last three months.
[P2]
Here's the idea.
First, I write the script and record the voice.
Then a small tool listens to every word and measures exactly when it starts.
With those timestamps, the animation knows where to move.
A chart grows on the word "growth", a box pops up right on the keyword, and nothing has to wait around for the voice to catch up.
[P3]
Actually, I never planned any of this.
I'm a researcher, not a designer, and at first I wasn't sure it would work at all.
But after a few weeks of trial and error, of breaking things and fixing them again, I realized that good timing matters more than fancy visuals.
Viewers rarely notice it when it's right, yet they always feel it when it's wrong.
[P4]
So, what does it take to make one of these?
Honestly, not much: a quiet room, a decent microphone, and a bit of patience.
If you'd like to try it yourself, the whole thing is free and open source.
Pick it up, play around with it, and let me know what you think.
